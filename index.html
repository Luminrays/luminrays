THE TECHNOLOGY

Light that behaves like living fire.

LuminRays is an electronic decorative flame system developed to reproduce the visual character of a real flame without combustion, smoke or heat. The technology is designed for architectural and atmospheric lighting.

LUMINRAYS

A different kind of light.

LuminRays combines electronics, optics and decorative design to create the feeling of fire without using an open flame. It is intended for locations where traditional fire is impractical, unsafe or simply not permitted.
